- `fonts/codicon.ttf` — [@vscode/codicons](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-codicons), © Microsoft Corporation, licensed CC BY 4.0.
  `codicons.txt` lists its glyphs' names, read from the font.
- `fonts/seti.ttf`, `vs-seti-icon-theme.json` — [Seti UI](https://github.com/jesseweed/seti-ui) icons as packaged in VS Code's `theme-seti` extension, © Jesse Weed, MIT License.
- `runtime\node.exe` (installer only) — [Node.js](https://nodejs.org), © OpenJS Foundation and Node.js contributors, MIT License;
  [its LICENSE](https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/main/LICENSE) also covers the libraries it bundles.
- `themes/dark-2026.json` — colors taken from VS Code's "Dark 2026" theme, © Microsoft Corporation, MIT License.
- `themes/dark-2026-colors.json` — VS Code's "Dark 2026" theme's colors with those it includes (Dark Modern, Dark+, Dark), for webviews' `--vscode-*` variables, © Microsoft Corporation, MIT License.
